❓ How to use

This calendar helps the group find days when everyone is free, then lock in a plan.

1. Log in

  • First time? Open Sign up, type your name and a password (twice).
  • Been here before? Open Log in, select your name, enter your password.
  • Your device remembers you until you Log out (Profile tab).

2. Read the calendar

Every day is coloured for the whole group:

  • Gold ⭐ everyone is free
  • Green more people free
  • Red more people busy
  • Purple 🎉 a hangout is confirmed
  • 3/5 = 3 of 5 people are free. ?2 and ✕1 = how many people aren't sure or are busy.
  • A small ? or ✕ badge is your own mark. A blue bar means the day has a note.

Days nobody marks count as free, so you only need to mark the days you can't do.

3. Tap a day

A menu pops up with:

  • ✅ Free, ❓ Not sure or ❌ Busy: your status for that day
  • 📝 Add note: write something about that day
  • 🎉 Plan hangout: start a confirmed plan on that day

To deselect a day, tap it again or press ✕ Deselect in the panel under the calendar.

The panel under the calendar shows who is free, not sure or busy that day, plus any notes and hangouts.

Change several days at once

  • Tap ☑️ Select days above the calendar, then tap each day you want. Tap a selected day again to unselect it.
  • ↔ Range: tap the first day, then the last day, and everything in between is selected (great for a holiday).
  • Weekends and All month select those days in the month you're looking at. You can switch months and keep selecting.
  • Then use the bar at the bottom: ✅ Free, ❓ Not sure, ❌ Busy, or 📝 Note to write one note for all the selected days.
  • To plan a hangout, tap a single day instead and choose 🎉 Plan hangout.

4. Notes 📝

  • Use From and To for a stretch of days, like "Abroad, back on the 5th". Leave To empty for one day.
  • Write anything: "Free after 6pm", "Can only do mornings".
  • You can add several notes to the same day. Everyone sees them, but only you can edit or delete yours.
  • Open All notes under the calendar to see them in one list.

5. Best dates

Under the calendar. It lists the days everyone is free, plus days that could work if the "not sure" people say yes. Tap one to jump to it.

6. Hangouts tab 🎉

Write down the plan once you've agreed: what, when, where and notes. Each hangout shows who wrote it and who edited it last. Anyone can edit or delete a hangout.

Tap a hangout to see its day in the calendar. Under each upcoming hangout you can also set your status (Free / Not sure / Busy) for that day, the same as marking it in the calendar.

7. Profile tab 👤

  • Change your name (your days, notes and hangouts move with it).
  • Change your password: type your old one, then the new one twice.
  • Copy invite link to send the group link to a friend.
  • Log out on this device.

Handy extras

  • Changes show up for everyone automatically. The line at the top says 🟢 Live when it's connected.
  • Forgot your password? Ask whoever set up the app. They can reset it in a minute.
